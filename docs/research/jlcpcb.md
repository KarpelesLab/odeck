# JLCPCB capabilities (researched 2026-10-02)

[V] = verified on jlcpcb.com, [C] = community/third-party, [I] = inference. Re-verify before ordering.

## Fab rules
- 0.09/0.09 mm trace/space, 0.15 mm min drill, via 0.15/0.25 mm [V].
- Via-in-pad (POFV, epoxy filled + capped) **free on 6–20 layers**, holes 0.15–0.55 mm [V].
- HDI 1–3 step, laser microvias 0.075–0.15 mm [V]. **Up to 24 layers in HDI mode** (user, 2026-10-02 — the "up to 8L" from the help FAQ is outdated).
- Back-drill 4–32L, holes 0.2–0.5 mm, +1–2 days [V].

## Assembly
- **Standard PCBA required** (Economic stops at 0.5 mm BGA / 0402) — BGA to 0.35 mm pitch, X-ray automatic [V].
- Double-sided supported (setup $51.12, stencil $16.42); extended parts $3/unique part [V].
- Standard PCBA min board 70×70 mm → 100×60 needs edge rails/panel [V/I].
- **Sourcing strategy (decided 2026-10-02):** parts not in LCSC stock are bought from Mouser/DigiKey and shipped to JLC as consigned parts.
- Consigned parts allowed (restrictions on mixing with stock) [V/C]; Global Sourcing for non-LCSC parts.
- **Programming: post-solder only, via an on-board interface, engineering-review, ~$7.86 + $7.86/h; no pre-programming of SPI flash** [V/C].
  → "works on arrival" must rely on ROM-boot chips or MCU self-flashing vendor SPI flash on first boot.

## Aesthetics
- Mask: green/purple/red/yellow/blue/white/black on all layer counts; matte black discontinued [V/C].
- **Full-color silkscreen only for 2–4L white boards, EasyEDA Pro only → not usable for us** [V].
  → Single-color silk + ENIG exposed-copper art; 6L+ ships ENIG.
- Silk min 0.15 mm line / 1.0 mm text height [V]. Castellations, edge plating available.
